Overview

CareCore is an AI-powered customer-care platform built around backend services, administrative operations, tenant-aware functionality and payment integration.Unverified

Described technology

  • Python and FastAPI, served with Uvicorn.
  • PostgreSQL through a configured database URL, with schema initialization at startup.
  • Groq for AI functionality and Stripe-related payment functionality.
  • Administrative authentication, a health endpoint and a dashboard route.
