CareCore

A configurable AI customer-care platform for conversations, orders and payments, with approvals before sensitive actions.

Open dashboard (opens in a new tab)

About

CareCore helps a business run customer care through an AI agent configured for its tone, products and policies. An admin dashboard gives the business a ledger of activity, an approvals queue and settings for how the agent behaves.

What it’s for

  • Configurable tone, scope handling, refund policy and escalation.
  • Approvals before sensitive actions, such as sending a payment link.
  • Escalation to a human by email, Slack webhook or SMS.
  • A product catalog and custom instructions for the agent.
