Overview

ELLA is an AI assistant for WhatsApp, built to hold natural, context-aware conversations instead of rigid helpdesk replies. It is meant to handle Nigerian English and Pidgin, and to work in both group and individual chats. The backend uses Node.js and Supabase.

Engineering focus

  • Routing that does not send an unrelated message to a specialized handler.
  • Honest failure handling when an AI provider or integration fails.
  • Keeping experimental voice-call work separate from production behavior.
